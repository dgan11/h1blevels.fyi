Shows how much above the DOL required minimum (prevailing wage) the company is actually paying.

1.0x = paying exactly the minimum
2.0x = paying double the minimum

DOL Wage Levels

Level I (17th %ile) - Entry-level, closely supervised
Level II (34th %ile) - Qualified, limited judgment
Level III (50th %ile) - Experienced, independent
Level IV (67th %ile) - Expert, supervisory

Note: The level reflects the job description filed, not actual pay. A Level II with $490K salary means they're paying 3x the required minimum - the level doesn't mean "junior."
